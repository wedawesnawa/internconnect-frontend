Monitoring and Evaluation (Monev) Application

Submitting a monitoring and evaluation (monev) application is an essential step for students in the internship process. This process ensures that progress and compliance with set objectives are systematically tracked. By completing a monev submission, students and mentors can collaboratively review the project milestones and make adjustments to meet the desired outcomes.
